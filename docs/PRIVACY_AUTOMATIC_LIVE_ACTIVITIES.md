# uFast privacy policy — automatic Live Activities release

**Effective date:** 9 August 2026

This release of uFast is a non-medical, local-only personal record-keeping app
for fasting, food, hydration and history.

## What uFast stores

uFast stores information you choose to enter: fasting intervals, food entries,
hydration entries, fasting and drink settings, and legacy history required by
the app's local schema. If you use Live Activities, uFast also stores the
automatic Live Activity preference and minimal presentation lifecycle metadata
needed to avoid duplicate requests and honour **Hide for this fast**.

## Where data stays

All of these records and presentation values remain on this iPhone in uFast's
protected local storage. uFast has no account, cloud sync, backup, restore,
export, import, analytics, advertising, tracking, HealthKit integration or
developer-operated backend. Live Activity requests are local ActivityKit
requests; they use no server, APNs or network connection.

## System-surface visibility

Live Activities are optional and default off. After a fast successfully starts,
uFast offers once to enable automatic Live Activities. If you choose to enable
them, elapsed time, goal progress and target may be visible on the Lock Screen
and Dynamic Island according to your iPhone's system settings. Each activity
stays active for up to eight hours. A longer still-active fast may receive a
new activity only when you later open or foreground uFast. You can turn the
setting off or choose **Hide for this fast** at any time.

## What uFast does not collect

No user-created record, preference, lifecycle value or Live Activity content is
sent to the developer or a third party. This feature does not introduce a new
off-device data collection category.

## Deletion

Settings provides **Delete all data**, which requires two explicit confirmations
and removes every uFast record, setting and Live Activity lifecycle value from
this iPhone. The action does not remove data outside uFast. uFast cannot recover
records deleted from the local store or removed with the app.

## Safety and purpose

uFast records user-entered information and displays patterns in those records.
It is not medical advice and does not diagnose, treat or guarantee a health
outcome. It does not infer that an absent record proves that you ate, drank or
fasted.

## Support and changes

If you voluntarily contact support, the information in that request is received
only to respond to you. Do not include information you do not want to share.
This policy may change when the app's data practices change. Private support is
available at <ufast.app@gmail.com>; public support is available at
<https://github.com/daveManDaveDude/uFast/issues>.
