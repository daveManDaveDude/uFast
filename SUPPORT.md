# uFast support

uFast 1.0 supports iPhone running iOS 26.0 or later. It is a free, local-only
manual tracker for fasting, food, hydration and history.

## Getting started

Choose a fasting goal on first launch, then use Today to start or correct a
fast, add food or drinks, and use History to review saved records. Settings
contains drink favourites, local-data information, privacy and safety content,
and **Delete all data**.

## Important limitation

uFast has no account, cloud sync, backup, restore or password recovery. Records
stay on the iPhone. Deleting the app or losing the iPhone may permanently lose
them.

For support, visit <https://github.com/daveManDaveDude/uFast/issues>. Please do
not include sensitive health information unless it is necessary to explain the
problem.
